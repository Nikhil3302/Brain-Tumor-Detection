Proposed Deep Learning Methodology

3.1 YOLO-NAS Detector

Optimized using Neural Architecture Search (NAS) to achieve superior single-stage localization. Predicts bounding boxes to pinpoint tumors regardless of shape, scale, or contrast variations, eliminating manual region selection.

3.2 ROI Extraction

Predicted bounding boxes isolate the pathological tissue from surrounding background noise. This ensures downstream classification concentrates solely on relevant neoplastic morphological features.

3.3 EfficientNet Classifier

Employs compound scaling balancing network depth, width, and resolution. Extracts fine-grained semantic attributes (texture, intensity, edges) to classify ROIs into Glioma, Meningioma, Pituitary, or Non-tumor.

Key Datasets Benchmarked
BraTS (Brain Tumor Segmentation) Multi-modal MRI scans with expert clinical annotations.
REMBRANDT Diverse clinical glioma and astrocytoma repository.
BR35H Tumor vs Non-Tumor MRI collection for CAD validation.
